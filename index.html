Mission StayFit – Fitness Studio in CIDCO N4, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar

Mission StayFit is a neighbourhood fitness studio in CIDCO N4, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra. We run small-group Yoga, Tabata and Zumba batches for all ages and fitness levels, so every member gets proper attention from the trainer.

Classes and batch timings

  • Yoga & Breathwork – Mon–Sat, 6:00–7:00 AM. All levels. Vinyasa, Hatha, Yin yoga and pranayama.
  • Tabata HIIT – Mon–Sat, 7:15–8:15 AM. Intermediate to advanced. 20 seconds on, 10 seconds off interval training.
  • Zumba Dance Fitness – Mon–Sat, 5:30–6:30 PM. All levels. Dance cardio to Latin and hip-hop music.
  • Navratri Garba Workshop – 28 Sept to 10 Oct. Hourly batches 11 AM–1 PM and 4–7 PM. ₹800 per person, ₹600 per person for a group of 5. Kids, teens and ladies welcome.
